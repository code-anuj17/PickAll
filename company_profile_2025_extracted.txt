Company Summary & Background 
 
PICKALL LOGISTICS PRIVATE LIMITED offers flexible and dedicated solutions for land transport 
support activities. Our management team, guided by Directors Rahul Sharma, is focused on 
bringing a professional, service-oriented approach to the logistics sector. 
We currently serve the critical logistics needs of clients like Tata Rewire and Resustainability 
Limited, carrying their consignments across the country with a starting fleet capacity of 5 
carriers. 
We value our customers and feel that customer service with customer delight is our first 
priority. Our commitment is to ensure the utmost care, with on-time and damage-free delivery 
as the core motto of our company. 
We aim to establish an excellent performance record and look forward to earning the 
appreciation of our partners as we grow. We employ strict tracking arrangements with high-
tech GPS systems to ensure safe and timely delivery.

ABOUT THE CARRIERS 
The company has the fleet of double Decker fully covered containerized Tata & Ashok 
Leyland trailers with perforated flooring which are hydraulically operated. These trailers 
are manufactured in such a size  to accommodate any type of car or any automobile 
vehicles combinations. Our body fabricators are Seamless Autotech. 
We are very much careful for the maintenance of hardware quality of the fleet. 
 
TRACKING ARRANGEMENTS 
 We have tracking arrangements with hi-tech GPS systems, which provides all types 
of en-route information about our vehicles. We share APIs with our customers for tracking 
their consignment too directly by them through internet. 
 Besides all above arrangements we are also maintaining our observer team for en- 
route checking to ensure the proper movement of the fleet.
